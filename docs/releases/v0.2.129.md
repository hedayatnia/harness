# Harness v0.2.129

Desktop release for macOS, Windows and Linux: agent messages from other chats
no longer look like your prompts, clicking anywhere in an inactive pane
activates it, and the empty composer's hint stays readable.

## Chats and panes

- A message one chat's agent sends to another chat shows as "Agent message ·
  sender", left-aligned, instead of looking like a prompt you typed. You can't
  open one in the composer from the queue (#445).
- Clicking, selecting text, or right- or middle-clicking in an inactive pane
  makes it active without losing the selection. The first click on a dropdown
  or on jump-to-latest also does its job (#198).
- An empty composer no longer fades its hint text, and leaving a question by
  switching chats brings back the normal "Do anything…" hint (#447).

## Agents

- Agents can rename a chat with the new `rename_chat` tool.
- When Harness stops a graff reply for its own reasons (replacing a run,
  shutting down, deleting a space), graff is told it wasn't the user. With
  graff 0.302.46 or later, the reply ends with "stopped by the client, not the
  user" instead of "interrupted by user".

## Terminal

- The fullscreen TUI shows session names and supports `/resume` with a
  searchable list of saved sessions.

Mobile builds are distributed separately; this desktop release does not itself
update TestFlight, App Store or Play installations.
